# BuildMind Cinematic V2 — Architectural Asset Brief

## Residence

Create one original illustrative contemporary tropical residence designed around the existing parcel and plan logic. It is marketing art, not a customer output or construction design.

### Massing and plan continuity

- Two-storey asymmetric composition with a grounded horizontal lower volume and lighter screened upper volume.
- Covered verandah facing the principal landscape edge.
- Living/dining space connected to the garden through shaded glazing.
- Stair and wet/service core remain consistent across plan, wireframe, exterior and interior.
- Roof, slab, window and door positions must derive from one authoritative model.
- Existing room labels and dimensions may be adjusted only through an approved data-model change in Phase 03.

### Material system

- Board-formed or smooth architectural concrete: restrained roughness variation, no exaggerated grunge.
- Timber: vertical screen/louvres and selected soffit surfaces, species represented generically unless provenance is supplied.
- Glass: physically plausible reflectance and interior visibility; avoid mirror-black glazing.
- Stone: locally plausible neutral aggregate/plinth, used as a grounding material rather than ornament.

### Landscape and light

- Tropical planting with a small number of botanically plausible species groups.
- Ground plane, pool/water and planting align with the parcel rather than forming a generic resort scene.
- Primary lighting: late-afternoon to blue-hour transition, sun from camera-left/rear.
- Warm interior light is a spatial cue, not an orange glow effect.

## Required shots

1. Exact registration camera for wireframe/material handoff.
2. Exterior hero, 28–35mm equivalent, 16:9 and 4:5 safe crops.
3. Exterior closer material study.
4. Living-area interior toward garden, 24–28mm equivalent.
5. Interior reverse or threshold shot showing continuity with exterior.
6. Orthographic or high-oblique plan reference.

Provide static fallback posters for each cinematic scene at desktop 1920×1080, tablet 1280×1024 and mobile 900×1200 working masters. Web derivatives are exported only after composition approval.

## Production methods

### A — Original in-house model and renders

- **Quality/continuity:** highest control and exact registration.
- **Licensing:** cleanest if employment/contract assignment is documented.
- **Time:** highest modelling and rendering effort.
- **Performance:** best control over web derivatives.
- **Verdict:** preferred.

### B — Licensed model adapted to BuildMind

- **Quality/continuity:** potentially high, but plan and proportions may resist the existing story.
- **Licensing:** requires proof of commercial web/render use, derivative rights and redistribution limits. Raw model must never ship if prohibited.
- **Time:** medium, with unpredictable cleanup.
- **Performance:** requires retopology and texture audit.
- **Verdict:** fallback only after exact-asset legal and technical review.

### C — AI-assisted illustrative imagery

- **Quality/continuity:** weakest geometric consistency across plan, wireframe, exterior and interior; correcting windows and massing is costly.
- **Licensing:** provider terms, training-data risk, provenance and human authorship must be documented.
- **Time:** fast for exploration, unreliable for final continuity.
- **Performance:** excellent once rendered to stills.
- **Verdict:** acceptable for mood exploration or controlled paint-over, not the authoritative final residence without substantial human reconstruction.

## Recommendation

Use **Method A**: one original model with an explicit rights assignment, plus pre-rendered exterior/interior media and an optimized technical derivative if needed. Method B may supply isolated vegetation or furniture only after licence review. Method C may assist mood exploration but should not define the final geometry.

## Deliverables and provenance

- Source model and renderer/version record
- Signed ownership or licence record
- Asset inventory with creator, source, date, permitted uses and restrictions
- Camera transforms and focal lengths
- Material list and texture sources
- Model/material ID renders for transition alignment
- Optimized web exports and SHA-256 hashes
- Accessibility descriptions and poster crops
- Written statement that imagery is illustrative

No assets are purchased, generated or downloaded in Phase 02.
